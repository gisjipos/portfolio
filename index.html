About me

From code to product leadership.

I’m a product-minded technology leader who enjoys turning complex problems into practical, user-centered products and helping teams move from ideas to outcomes.

I started my career as a Web Developer at Wela School Systems, building hands-on technical experience and learning how software decisions affect real users.

I later moved into product as a Product Owner, where I focused on defining needs, aligning teams, improving processes, and shaping solutions around customer value.

That journey eventually led me to become the Chief Product Officer of Livro Systems Inc., where I lead with a mix of technical understanding, product thinking, collaboration, and purpose.
